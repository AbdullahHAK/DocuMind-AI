## Pre-existing conditions

Under the ACA, health plans can't refuse to cover you or charge you more just because you have a pre-existing condition (a health problem you had before coverage started, such as asthma, diabetes, cancer or pregnancy). They also can't refuse to cover treatment for that condition once your coverage starts. Grandfathered individual plans and short-term health plans are exceptions.

## Young adults up to age 26

If a plan offers dependent coverage, it must let children stay on a parent's plan until they turn 26, whether or not they are married, living with their parents, financially dependent, or eligible for their own employer's coverage.

## Rescissions

A plan can't cancel your coverage retroactively (a "rescission") just because you made an honest mistake on your application. Rescission is only allowed for fraud or intentional misrepresentation, or for failure to pay premiums, and the insurer must give 30 days' advance notice.
